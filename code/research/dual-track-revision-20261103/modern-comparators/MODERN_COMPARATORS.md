# Prospective modern-comparator recommendation — 2026-11-03

Status: proposal for independent review, not an accepted protocol or performance result. No channels, optimization cells, or performance outcomes were generated. All writes are confined to this directory.

## Decision

Recommend two **adapted optimization components**, subject to correctness review: **Fu-SCA (TCOM 2026, adapted; exact denominator)** and **Thu-IDE (TCOM 2026 accepted manuscript, adapted power search)**. Keep strong common-model WMMSE and direct multistart SLSQP in the new rate study, and preserve R1 Full's complete legacy certification evidence. Neither adapter reproduces its source communication system. A plot can compare these implementations on our common model; it cannot establish that our architecture outperforms either source paper's architecture, published curves, or entire algorithm.

Do **not** create a second purportedly independent modern comparator by renaming WMMSE after Ou. In the Shannon limit, the transferable part of Ou's algorithm reduces algebraically to the existing WMMSE amplitude block. Its distinctive finite-blocklength transformation disappears. Chen's coupled hybrid-beamforming and beam-squint method is not recommended for numerical adaptation here.

The recommended two additions are genuinely different searches: Fu maximizes a concave log-of-SINR-minorant in amplitudes; Thu uses two adaptively selected differential-evolution mutations, an archive, and success-weighted parameter adaptation. Direct SLSQP of our original nonconcave objective must remain a separate control. A numerical optimizer may solve Fu's convex subproblem, but the source-derived surrogate and its iterations must actually be implemented.

## Fu power-block adaptation: executable mathematical specification

For the current assignment and positive p, choose unit-norm MMSE directions w_i and rotate their phases so w_i^H h_i is real nonnegative. Define b_i=|w_i^H h_i|, c_ij=|w_i^H h_j|^2 for another user on the same resource, zero otherwise; c_ii=0; n_i=||w_i||^2=1. Let v_i=sqrt(p_i), psi_i=b_i v_i and N_i(v)=n_i+sum_j c_ij v_j^2. For old vbar, a_i=b_i vbar_i/N_i(vbar).

The transferable Eq.(25c) tangent is

`t_i(v) = 2 a_i b_i v_i - a_i^2 N_i(v)`.

Solve the convex subproblem

`maximize H(v)=sum_i ln(1+t_i(v))`

subject to sqrt(.05)<=v_i<=sqrt(.2), sum v_i^2<=.8, and t_i(v)>=0. At vbar, t_i=gamma_i. Globally t_i <= (b_i v_i)^2/N_i(v), because the gap equals `(b_i*v_i-a_i*N_i(v))^2/N_i(v)`. Thus the surrogate is tight and gradient-matched at the old point, concave on its feasible domain, and produces a valid common-model ascent step when solved accurately. Recomputed MMSE can only improve the fixed-receiver Shannon sum rate. The t>=0 domain is an explicitly added conservative inner domain, feasible at every current nonnegative-SINR iterate; zero-gain users contribute zero and can be omitted from H.

Gradient for implementation: `dH/dv_j = sum_i [2*a_i*b_i*1(i=j)-2*a_i^2*c_ij*v_j]/(1+t_i)`. Jacobian of each t uses the same numerator. Sum-budget gradient is2v. Stable log1p must be used. SciPy SLSQP may minimize -H with these analytic derivatives and convex constraints, warm-starting at vbar; it is only the subproblem engine. Validate feasibility, surrogate ascent and true MMSE-rate ascent; reject a bad numerical step and retain the incumbent. Deadline termination is not convergence. Recommended subproblem ftol1e-10, emergency maxiter500; if time remains after iteration limit, resume rather than intentionally truncate the comparator. Outer stop requires both small true-rate change and a documented residual; otherwise report deadline or stalled numerical solve.

**Printed-source defect:** Eq.(25e) on p.6449 prints `pi_k >= pi_hat_k + sum_{i!=k} rho_i V_ik rho_i^T + sigma_k^2`. Algorithm2 initializes pi_hat as the full interference-plus-noise. Literal use double-counts the old denominator and breaks tangency. The adaptation therefore retains the verified quadratic-over-linear tangent (25c), but substitutes the exact common-model N_i(v) for the source denominator. This is a disclosed modification, not a silent OCR correction or verbatim reproduction of P2.2. It also omits the regularization block(24), DE fixed-point machinery, and source QoS/per-EDU constraints.

## Thu IDE power-search adaptation

Implement Eqs.(31)–(36) and Algorithm1's generation loop explicitly; do not call a stock fixed-parameter `differential_evolution` and label it IDE. Optimize normalized powers z_i=(p_i-.05)/.15 for a fixed assignment, using the common wrapper for assignment exchange. DimensionD=K=8, population Q=min(4D,300)=32. Include common p0, identity/uniform and the common deterministic random starts in that order, deduplicating exact duplicates; fill the remaining population with independent uniform z draws and the same feasibility projection. Common starts are reused without free local polishing. Initialize mu_F=.2, mu_CR=1, lambda=.2, T=10 generations, matching Sec.V. Maximum500 generations is an emergency/restart boundary within the same deadline, never extra time.

Use top10% (ceil(.1Q), at least2) as the pbest pool; Cauchy/normal scale .1; archive sizeQ; these three unspecified source details are **prospective implementation conventions**, not claims of exact published settings. Separate mu_F/mu_CR and success lists by mutation operator as source prose specifies. Each generation uses a frozen old population and produces the next population; archive displaced successful parents, removing uniform random archive entries above Q.

For each parent x, choose operator1 with probability lambda. Let xpbest be uniform from the top pool, r1 uniform from the old population excluding the parent, and r2 uniform from old population plus archive excluding the parent and r1 identity. Operator1: `m=xpbest+F*(r1-r2)`. Operator2: `m=x+F*(xpbest-x)+F*(r1-r2)`. Sample F from Cauchy(mu_F,.1), resampling while <=0 and clipping above1; sample CR from Normal(mu_CR,.1) clipped to[0,1]. Binomial crossover selects each mutant coordinate with probabilityCR and forces a uniformly selected coordinate to come from the mutant. A forced coordinate does not mathematically ensure a distinct trial when vectors coincide.

Project trial power to the common box/shared budget: `p_i=clip(raw_i-tau,.05,.2)`, tau>=0 found by bisection so sum<=.8, tau=0 if the clipped vector already fits. Apply exact rational repair before publishing; store the repaired phenotype back as z to prevent genotype/evaluation mismatch. This changes source bound-only feasibility handling because our sum constraint is essential. Selection is strict true-rate improvement after candidate feasibility checks. Successful gains determine normalized weights in each operator's Lehmer updates `mu=sum(w*s^2)/sum(w*s)` for both F and CR. Empty success set or zero denominator retains the previous mean.

Every10 generations update lambda to.8 if operator1's gain per invocation exceeds operator2's, else.2; reset window totals. Count CE on **all attempted operator calls**, following source Sec.IV-B2 prose; Algorithm1 line16 increments CE only in the successful branch, an ambiguity that is disclosed and fixed prospectively. With an untried operator, retain lambda until both have calls; do not divide by zero. Gain and CE bookkeeping is a required fixture. Reset population/archive for a new assignment, seed from the same documented common starts, and charge every evaluation. No further hyperparameter tuning on pilot or holdout outcomes.

## Why Ou is not an extra arm

With M_i=b_i^2 v_i^2 and fixed-receiver N_i, source lambda_i=M_i/N_i and z_i=sqrt((1+lambda_i)M_i)/(M_i+N_i). In the infinite-blocklength limit, the Eq.(73) dispersion auxiliary drops out. The remaining Eq.(74) maximizes `sum_i [2*z_i*sqrt(1+lambda_i)*b_i*v_i-z_i^2*(M_i+N_i)]`. This is a separable quadratic in amplitudes with the same common-multiplier solution as `_amplitude_update` in existing WMMSE. Recomputing MMSE makes its coefficient identification exact. Keeping the source's power-independent receiver would make it artificially disadvantaged under our common receiver rule. It may be cited as an equivalent Shannon-limit algorithmic connection, but must not inflate the number of distinct modern comparisons or hide that its FBL contribution is absent.
