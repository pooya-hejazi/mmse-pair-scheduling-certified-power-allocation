# Price-guided joint assignment and power selection

Decision recorded 11 November 2026, before any new holdout optimization. Archived 10 November outcomes are development data for this extension. The two development blocks 0 and 1 cover all nine native CDL conditions and are excluded from confirmation. Keep the previous complete experiment intact.

## Method and comparisons

Interference and a shared power budget couple assignment to power. The proposed search evaluates candidate powers for each candidate pair at common nonnegative budget prices, optimizes the resource-labelled assignment on the resulting pair weights, repairs feasible powers and polishes the resulting schedules with the existing analytic-gradient SQP step. Check every retained incumbent against the same feasible set and recomputed-MMSE rate. A grid price is a proposal mechanism. Any floating upper diagnostic must be identified as numerical. Report the actual finite work, warmup, failures, and all retained allocations.

Control arms are DP–SQP, DP–Fu–SCA and DP–Thu–IDE. All four arms use the same accelerated exact fixed-power assignment kernel where applicable, eight shared feasible starts, single-thread numerical libraries, a 10-s allowance inclusive of algorithm-specific work and a maximum 5-s local power block. Each method may stop earlier when its work is exhausted. Exclude shared case preparation, one-time generic JIT compilation and final independent replay from per-case timing; record them separately. Use serial, cyclically interleaved method order. Save results per case and arm, including deadline/stall diagnostics; do not discard losses. Compare against the stronger of the actual outputs even when a comparator beats the proposed method.

## Independent population and endpoints

Draw twenty fresh root replicates. Each has three independently generated CDL-A/B/C user populations evaluated at nominal 0, 10 and 20 dB. The channels, model, powers, normalization, K=16, S=8, M=2 and SNR dependence match the original independent study. Exclude every recorded historical, previous main and development seed from the new effective seed set. All comparisons within a case share its channels, constraints and offered starts. The new data stand alone; do not pool them with November 10 cases for testing. Freeze executable experiment settings before evaluating the holdout.

**Primary rate endpoint.** On each case subtract the maximum achieved occupied-band SE among the three controls from the new method's SE. Average equally over the nine conditions within a replicate, then over twenty replicates. Form a two-sided 95% percentile interval with 20,000 resamplings of whole replicates. Call a rate improvement material only if its lower endpoint exceeds the study-defined 0.01 bit/s/Hz margin. Report individual control contrasts, all W/T/L counts at 0.00001 bit/s/Hz, and cell effects regardless of sign.

**Secondary computational endpoint.** Require jointly (i) the lower endpoint of a two-sided 97.5% block interval for the same rate contrast above −0.001 bit/s/Hz and (ii) the upper endpoint of a two-sided 97.5% block interval for mean log search time (new method divided by the fastest control in each case) below log(0.5). The two individual intervals provide a conservative nominal 95% family within this secondary assessment; interpret the exponentiated estimate as a geometric mean of paired time ratios. The practical cutoffs are study choices. This result, if it passes, supports rate-preserving search efficiency under the measured warm-runtime conditions. The single-host timing design leaves cross-host variability unmeasured. The two studies' original and accelerated control timings must remain labelled separately.
