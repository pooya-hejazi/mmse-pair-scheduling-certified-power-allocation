Search coverage included fixed-antenna MMSE scheduling hardness, rational channel orthogonality/3DM, two-user power polynomial pricing, and exact curvature/envelope terms. It was not exhaustive. The exact threshold and 17-candidate expression were not located in inspected sources; this remains a scoped observation, not priority proof. The new Mostafa source is a concrete missing full-text item; the user's four supplied Ruder/Fan PDFs are already available and do not need to be requested again.

The current promising combination is a fixed-dimensional complexity boundary plus an all-correlation continuous pricing certificate. A publishable paper still needs a nonroutine contribution beyond these supporting lemmas or a quantitatively substantial algorithmic consequence, an appropriate physical model, matched baselines, and reproducible evaluation. No claimed “6G framework” is justified by a label alone.
