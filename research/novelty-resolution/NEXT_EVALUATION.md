This is a proposed dependent packet, not permission to bypass the unaccepted scientific-stage gates or reset experiment/review budgets. Preserve all negative selective-CSI findings and the fixed estimator. The current evidence supports pursuing continuous optimization certificates more strongly than claiming selective-information throughput gains.

| Method | Required matching and interpretation |

| Recent related algorithms | Implement only after full-text receiver/objective/constraints reconcile. Different SIC or minimum-power/QoS models are context comparisons, not interchangeable same-model baselines. |

Required performance endpoints: feasible throughput and per-user rates; radiated power and throughput-versus-power curve; continuous optimality-gap upper bound; failure/timeout rate; median and tail end-to-end runtime; time to a shared target gap. Use paired instances and paired uncertainty intervals in the accepted confirmatory design. Report certificate benefit even when throughput is unchanged, but require meaningful computational or accuracy benefit before a performance superiority claim.

GPU question: exact Fraction/SymPy root isolation in this prototype is CPU software. A defensible GPU route is batched channel Gram matrices and floating candidate evaluation, followed by CPU certification or verified GPU interval bounds. It must include transfer and certification costs, memory limits, warm-up and precision; compare against a vectorized CPU implementation. Current results justify no GPU speedup or scalable exact ILP claim. Profile first; do not port a small symbolic loop merely to attach a GPU label.

Go/no-go: advance a theory paper only if a specific, nonroutine result remains different from the nearest full-text work and the application assumptions are defensible. Advance a performance paper only with a matched, reproducible advantage or useful certification tradeoff. If neither survives, record the negative finding and revisit the model; do not expand to generic 6G constraints or estimator training to obscure the failed claim. The requested formal >=30-paper table and final prose follow system/evaluation, not precede them.
