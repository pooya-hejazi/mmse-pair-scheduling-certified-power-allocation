## Timing repair

Every worker runs in its own process. Parent enforces a wall-clock deadline that includes interpreter/import/setup/load/solve/save, records PID/status and kills its directly launched worker if expired. Workers cannot launch child processes. Incumbents and stage records are atomically persisted before/after each solver call; a timeout may retain a feasible incumbent but never an optimum claim. Arm wall limits20s including setup for fixed-power, SLSQP continuous power, adapted I-WMMSE, certificate-stop I-WMMSE, grid MILP, and LP reference. Uniform-power bisection minimizes power at95% of the common schedule's fixed-power rate; compare previous min-grid power only when targets/schedules match, otherwise no savings claim.

The I-WMMSE specialization uses MMSE receivers, inverse scalar MSE weights, then a convex quadratic amplitude update with a common sum-power multiplier and box clipping. Bounds are the same as SLSQP/grid. Max500 iterations, relative objective stopping1e-8. SLSQP max500 iterations, ftol1e-10; retain only feasible nondecreasing incumbents. They are local continuous methods, not global or original-paper reproductions. A no-interference water-filling upper bound is valid for continuous power; the LP upper bound is only valid for its finite grid. Certificate early stop uses relative gap<=1% against the continuous upper bound. This threshold is recorded before results and is an approximation guarantee for nominal known CSI, not proof of exactness or novelty.
