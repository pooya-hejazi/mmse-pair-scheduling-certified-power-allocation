# Attribution correction to the frozen exactness-boundary note

31 October 2026. This addendum does not modify the independently reviewed PROOF_NOTE.md or its frozen hashes.

Primary source: Steven Kelk and Georgios Stamoulis, *Integrality Gaps for Colorful Matchings*, [arXiv:1801.07937](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.07937), [Discrete Optimization 32 (2019), 73–92](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disopt.2018.12.003). Full text inspected in the targeted screen; see ASTRA_SCREEN.md for details.

Their vertex/color-budget LP is equation (1), pp.4–5. The text explains that a gap can survive blossom inequalities. The alternating bichromatic four-cycle is discussed on p.13. Section 4.1, pp.19–20, treats its four-edge inequality; Lemma 3 derives it within two Sherali–Adams rounds. Consequently neither the combinatorial obstruction nor that cut is a new contribution of this project.

Our four-user cycle maps to vertex order 1–2–4–3–1, with edges 12 and 34 assigned resource/color 0, and 24 and 13 resource/color 1. Completing the eligible graph and using rate-derived weights changes the optimization problem: our exact user/resource cover is not their maximum-cardinality packing. The high-weight half-integral cycle still explains the obstruction, but their gap ratio 2 must not be transferred to our positive baseline rates. The project’s physical construction supplies the separate weighted-gap calculation.

The rational near-orthogonal channel realization and restricted ideal-threshold hardness argument require their own priority comparison. General colorful-matching hardness does not establish novelty of a physical embedding; conversely it does not by itself prove the detailed embedding was published before. The growing receive dimension M=K² and arbitrary resource-dependent vectors limit the wireless interpretation.

Updated research judgment: the current bundle is unsuitable as a main novelty claim without an additional substantial, independently checked contribution. The earlier technical review remains evidence about the unchanged algebra, not clearance of originality. No claim is made that every possible exactness-boundary result is already known.
