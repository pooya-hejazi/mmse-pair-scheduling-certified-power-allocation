For each four-user subset and two distinct resources, enumerate the three perfect pair partitions. Put two different partitions on the two resources; the four columns are pairwise conflicting. Add their sum <=1 to the original equalities and ALL projected odd-set cuts. This is a classical set-packing clique inequality. Its family is polynomial in K,S, but no claim that it describes the integer hull. With powers as columns the present implementation does not apply unchanged.

Validate the combinatorial clique condition for every generated row; reproduce the old odd-set LP and compare its value to the frozen reference; solve the strengthened LP. Objective equality to an independently available IP is an instance certificate, not a proof that the relaxation is always integral.
