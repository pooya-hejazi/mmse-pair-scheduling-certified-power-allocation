## Reflection 2 — Mathematical correctness and edge cases: PASS

1. **MMSE and rational realization.** Sherman–Morrison gives the stated SINRs, and factoring each 1+SINR yields the pair-rate identity. The private and pair coordinates produce exactly K² coordinates, equal squared norm 1+(K−1)t², and inner product either zero or t². Positivity/PSD is guaranteed by the explicit vectors. Arbitrarily small positive rational t is legitimate; no irrational factorization or singularity is hidden. Independent exact checks include t=1/1,000,000 and t=3/2, and direct complex-MMSE inversions agree within 8.9e−16.

2. **Four-user gap and projected odd sets.** The four preferred columns are a conflict clique. Every labeled integer schedule contains at most one of them, and some contain one. The half-weight witness satisfies all user/resource equalities and all four nontrivial odd-set inequalities. The constant upper rate and total column mass prove optimality, rather than merely feasibility of the witness. Exact rational A>B>0 verifies positivity without floating-point cancellation. Independently solved LP penalty is 0 and exhaustive integer penalty is 1. Singleton odd subsets are trivial; there are no additional nontrivial odd subsets at K=4.

3. **3DM encoding and complexity.** A schedule attains the ideal product iff every selected pair encodes a triple, hence iff it is a perfect 3DM. All pairs remain feasible; forbidden pairs are separated by objective value, not silently deleted. With t=1/K the dense channel output has O(K⁴) rational entries across resources, each with polynomial encoding length. The rational threshold (1+g)^K has polynomial bit length; a finite schedule certificate permits polynomial-bit rational SINR/product verification. The standard NP-completeness of perfect 3DM is the explicitly assumed source theorem. The fixed-power lower bound follows from g<2, −ln(1−x)≥x, and x=t⁴/(1+g)²≥1/(9K⁴). No approximation claim beyond inverse-polynomial additive separation follows. Independent enumeration agrees for all 256 n=2 inputs (175 yes), 60 n=3 inputs, eight n=4 inputs, and both n=1 inputs. These checks support, rather than replace, the general reduction.

4. **Continuous-power corollary.** For nonnegative interference, γ_u≤g p_u; strict concavity together with monotonicity and the total budget forces p_u=p_bar at threshold equality. Because both scheduled powers are positive, equality in the SINR bound forces orthogonality of every pair. A yes instance attains the threshold with the admissible uniform power vector. There are finitely many schedules and a compact power domain, so a no instance has a strictly smaller maximum. This proves hardness of ideal-threshold attainment, not arbitrary-threshold NP membership or the fixed-power numerical gap for variable powers. The project's physical normalization is asserted, not independently validated here. Exact finite-grid checks on the four-user no instance tested 396 schedule/power combinations and all were strictly below the ideal product.

5. **Additive bound.** Projecting any configuration LP point to user/resource marginals gives a feasible capacity-two transportation assignment; this suffices for LP*≤Q0. The transportation optimum is integral, and all-pair eligibility makes its recovered pairing feasible. Subtracting at most max_e δ_es on each resource gives the stated bound. The hypotheses exclude extra coupled constraints or variable-power coupling unless separately proved. Independent exhaustive IP, LP, odd-LP, and assignment comparisons on 24 random complex 6-user/3-resource channel tables satisfy every bound within 8.9e−16.

6. **Clique cut.** Two distinct perfect pair partitions on four users have no common edge; every edge from one partition intersects every edge from the other. Columns on a common resource also conflict. Consequently the four columns are pairwise incompatible, making their sum≤1 valid. On the witness the sum is 2. Adding this one cut independently restores the correct witness objective (penalty 1). This proves neither general integrality nor useful practical improvement.
