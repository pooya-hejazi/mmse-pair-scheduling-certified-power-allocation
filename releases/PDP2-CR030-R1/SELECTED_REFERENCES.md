# Five selected papers

## Our papers (our_papers.bib)
1. P. Hejazi and M. Sabaei, Optimizing Spectral Efficiency through User Clustering in User-centric Cell-free Massive MIMO Environment for 6G Wireless Network, JCN28(1),30–39,2026. DOI10.23919/JCN.2025.000085. Clustering/BS association with SIC; distinguished from this paper's no-SIC common receiver.
2. P. Hejazi, M. Sabaei and A. Hajmahmoudi, Pilot-Efficient Channel Estimation for 6G MU-mMIMO Using LS Initialization and GAN-Augmented CNN Refinement, JCN28(3),401–408,2026. DOI10.23919/JCN.2025.000112. Pilot/CSI estimation; distinguished from certification for fixed nominal channels.

## Three closest external papers (closest_other_papers.bib)
1. Fan et al., Joint User Pairing and Resource Allocation for LTE Uplink Transmission, IEEE TWC11(8),2838–2847,2012. DOI10.1109/TWC.2012.070912.111305. Closest MMSE-aware pair/resource allocation; prescribed powers.
2. Viterbo and Hottinen, Optimal User Pairing for Multiuser MIMO, ISSSTA,242–246,2008. DOI10.1109/ISSSTA.2008.50. Direct MMSE disjoint-pair scheduling predecessor; equal powers/resource-invariant channels.
3. Shen and Yu, Fractional Programming for Communication Systems—Part II: Uplink Scheduling via Matching, IEEE TSP66(10),2631–2644,2018. DOI10.1109/TSP.2018.2812748. Joint matching/power/beamforming predecessor; stationarity differs from a global integral bound.
